Privacy Policy

The short version: we collect nothing about you, store nothing about you, and share nothing about you. Last updated: September 10, 2026.

The whole point of strngr

strngr pairs you with a random stranger for a video or text conversation. It exists on one principle: a conversation between two strangers is nobody else's business — including ours.

What we collect: nothing

  • No accounts. No email, phone number, or sign-up of any kind.
  • No identifiers. You are a random socket connection in memory. Even we can't tell who you are.
  • No message storage. Text messages are relayed to your partner and immediately discarded. There is no chat history — anywhere.
  • No video storage. Your camera and mic never touch our servers. Video/audio is peer-to-peer (WebRTC), encrypted with DTLS-SRTP. It flows directly from your device to the other person's device.
  • No logs that identify you. We keep no IP logs tied to conversations, no timestamps of who talked to whom, and no analytics.
  • No trackers, cookies, or fingerprinting. The site sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts, fonts, or analytics.

The server's matchmaking state lives in RAM and is wiped on every restart. There is no database. If the server restarted mid-conversation, it would be physically impossible for anyone to recover what was said.

What the other person can see

Whatever you choose to show or type. This is a live conversation with a real human being — the same rules as talking to a stranger on the street apply. Please be decent. Please be careful.

Reporting

Tap Report & skip to end a chat and flag the other user. Reports are counted anonymously in memory for 10 minutes — we never learn your identity, and the other person is not told who reported them. Users accumulating reports are disconnected from their chats.

Age and legality

strngr is 18+. You are responsible for the content of your conversation and for following your local laws. Do not share personal information with strangers: no real names early on, no addresses, no financial details, no intimate photos of yourself or anyone else.

How we protect the data we don't have

  • All traffic is served over HTTPS/WSS with TLS.
  • Media is encrypted in transit (DTLS-SRTP) between peers.
  • Messages are rendered as plain text on the client — HTML/links are never executed, so a stranger can't send you a malicious link that does anything.
  • Signaling payloads are size-capped and rate-limited to prevent abuse.

Children

strngr is not for anyone under 18. Because there is no sign-up, we rely on honest use. If a minor is found using the service, their connection is simply another socket we may disconnect.

Contact

Questions or abuse reports with details: strngr.app@outlook.com. We read everything. We store nothing from your email beyond replying to it.

Deliberately kept short. A privacy policy you can read in two minutes is a feature, not a bug.
