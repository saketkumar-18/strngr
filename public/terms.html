Be a decent human. The rest is detail. Last updated: September 10, 2026.

1. What this is

strngr randomly pairs you with another person for video or text conversation. By using it you confirm you are 18 or older. If you're not, close this tab.

2. The golden rule

The person on the other side is real. Don't do anything to a stranger here you wouldn't do to a stranger on the street:

  • No nudity or sexual content involving minors — zero tolerance, and it's a crime.
  • No harassment, hate speech, threats, or targeted abuse.
  • No flashing, sexual harassment, or unsolicited explicit content.
  • No recording or screenshotting people without their consent, where laws require it.
  • No spam, phishing links, scams, malware, or advertising.
  • No impersonation of others.

Every chat has a Report & skip button. Use it. Users accumulating reports get disconnected.

3. No warranty, no recording by us

Chats are not stored by us and never will be. Because we store nothing, we also can't moderate content after the fact, retrieve a conversation, or act as arbiter in "he said, she said" disputes. Moderation is community-driven via reports at the moment they happen. The service is provided "as is," with no warranty of any kind. Use the Skip and Report buttons; protect yourself.

4. Privacy by design

See the Privacy Policy — no accounts, no logs, no trackers, peer-to-peer video.

5. Your responsibility

You are responsible for what you say, show, and share. Don't reveal personal information to strangers — no real name, address, school, financial details, or intimate media. We can't undo what you send; the other person sees it in real time.

6. Availability & changes

The service may go down, restart, or change at any time (it's free). If it restarts, all state is wiped — that's a feature of the privacy model, not a bug.

7. Termination

We can disconnect anyone for any reason, especially for conduct in section 2. Because there are no accounts, "banning" means dropping your connection; repeat abusers are handled at the network level.
